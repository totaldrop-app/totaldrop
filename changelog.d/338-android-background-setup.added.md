- **Android:** At first start TotalDrop asks you to let it run without
  battery restrictions, so that a transfer keeps going when you switch
  apps or lock the screen. It opens Android's battery settings and, on
  phones whose maker adds a battery saver of its own (Xiaomi, Samsung and
  others), that saver too. If a transfer goes to the background while the
  restrictions are still on, the page comes back once; it is always in
  Settings (#338).
