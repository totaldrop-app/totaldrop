# Getting help

- **Something does not work:** search the [open issues](https://github.com/totaldrop-app/totaldrop/issues). If it is not there, [report a bug](https://github.com/totaldrop-app/totaldrop/issues/new/choose) with your system, the app's version (Settings) and what you did.
- **An idea or a missing feature:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/totaldrop-app/totaldrop/issues/new/choose). Check the [roadmap](https://totaldrop.app/roadmap/) first.
- **A security problem:** never in public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **A translation that reads wrong:** see [docs/ceviri.md](docs/ceviri.md), or open an issue that names the language and the sentence.
- **The Android beta, press, anything else:** **hello@totaldrop.app**.

TotalDrop is run by volunteers and paid for by donations, so answers can take a few days. Thank you for your patience.
