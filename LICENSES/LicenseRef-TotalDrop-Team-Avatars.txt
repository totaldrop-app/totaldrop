The pictures in site/public/team/avatars/ are the GitHub profile pictures
of the people shown on the TotalDrop team page. Each belongs to the person
it shows (or to whoever holds its rights) and is used on totaldrop.app with
that person's permission. They are not covered by the project's licenses
and may not be reused outside this repository and its web site without the
permission of the person concerned. A person who no longer wants to be
shown can ask the maintainer, and the picture is removed.
