# Code of conduct

TotalDrop is built by people from many countries, in many languages, with every level of experience. This is how we treat each other in issues, pull requests, reviews, translations and any other place the project gathers.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Critique work, never people. Say what is wrong and how it could be better.
- Make room for newcomers and for people writing in a second language.
- Respect privacy. Never share someone's personal details, messages or location without their consent. For a privacy project this matters twice.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on what goes into the project, and discuss it calmly if you disagree.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, threats, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind, including about nationality, language, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or appearance.
- Sexual content or advances.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or flooding issues with noise.
- Publishing a security vulnerability before it is fixed (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks these rules, in proportion to what happened. Serious or repeated harm leads to a permanent ban.

## Reporting

Write to **hello@totaldrop.app** with "Conduct" in the subject, or contact a maintainer privately on GitHub. Reports are read only by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within a week. If the report concerns a maintainer, say so and another maintainer handles it.

This code applies in every project space, and in public when someone represents the project.
